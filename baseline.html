SyllaBuddy

Version 3: paste syllabus text, get an editable list of assignments, exams, readings, dates, and due times, then export it to your calendar. This version uses simple rules, not AI. It is the non-AI baseline the AI version will be compared against.

Default due time: for example 23:59 if this course closes at 11:59 PM. Items with no time of their own get it. Readings are left blank because their cutoff is often different.

The calendar file saves times without a time zone, so they show in your calendar's own time zone. Rows with no valid date are left out.
