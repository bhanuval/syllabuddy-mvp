SyllaBuddy

Paste or upload your classes. AI builds one schedule from that input: type, date, due time, and the source line. You review every row.

Default due time: for example 23:59 if this course closes at 11:59 PM. Items with no time of their own get it. Readings stay blank unless the syllabus gives a reading cutoff.

    Timed items become events that end at the due time. Rows with no valid date are left out of the calendar.

    Highlighted rows need review: missing date, missing time, a weekday that does not match the date, or a time that may be a class meeting. Check every row against the pasted syllabus. Exam and quiz stay separate from assignment because they weigh more on the grade.

    Privacy: paste only text you are allowed to use. The pasted text or uploaded files are sent to our server and then to OpenAI so AI can extract items. The API key stays on the server. This page does not require an account. Nothing is saved on the server; your list is stored only in this browser.
