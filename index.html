SyllaBuddy

Big semester. Little buddy.

Turn a syllabus into one reviewed schedule you can export to your calendar. The AI drafts it. You check every row.

1Add your syllabus

Drop files here, or click Upload files. You can add several: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or screenshots. A screenshot works well when a PDF will not.

    Optional: course name and default due time

    Default due time: for example 23:59 if this course closes at 11:59 PM. Items with no time of their own get it. Readings stay blank unless the syllabus gives a reading cutoff.

    2Build your schedule

    We restored your list from earlier in this tab.

    3Review, then export

    AI draft

    Click any cell to edit it. Highlighted rows have a note in the Check column and need your decision. Readings and class meetings do not need a due time. Tick Reviewed when a row is right.

    Course Type Item Date Time Origin Source text Details Link Check
    More tools: sort, add a row, apply a default time, clear

    Timed items become events that end at the due time. Rows with no valid date are left out of the calendar.

    Privacy: paste only text you are allowed to use. The pasted text or uploaded files are sent to our server and then to OpenAI so AI can extract items. The API key stays on the server. This page does not require an account. Nothing is saved on the server. Your list stays in this browser tab so a refresh does not lose it, and it is cleared when you close the tab.
